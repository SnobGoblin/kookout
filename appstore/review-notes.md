## Notes (paste this; max 4000 characters)

Kookout tells wind, wave and snow sports people when their own spots have good conditions, judged by each spot's own rules (wind directions, wind strength, wave height, fresh snow), and sends a push notification when good conditions come up or change.

No account is needed. Sign in with Apple is only used to share spots with other users and to fix the name or position of shared spots. Any Apple ID works, so no demo account is needed.

How to try it:
1. Tap + to add a spot. The map shows popular spots; you can also search for a place or drop a pin anywhere.
2. Tap a spot to see its hourly forecast. Green hours are good by that spot's rules. Tap Edit to change the wind directions and limits.
3. Notifications are checked hourly on our server, so a real one can take a while. To see one at once: Settings (gear icon) → Send a test notification.

User-generated content (guideline 1.2):
- Users can share spots (a name and a map position) and fix the name or position of shared spots. Every change is logged with the user's public name and can be undone by anyone signed in.
- Every spot, and every change in its history, has "Report a problem" (reasons: wrong, private, offensive, other).
- Reports are emailed to us and normally handled by hand within 24 hours. We remove offending content and can block an account, which stops it from signing in and editing.
- Contact for users: hi@kookout.no, also on https://kookout.no/privacy.html.

The app is free; there are no in-app purchases in this version.

Forecasts and observations come from public open-data services: MET Norway, the Norwegian Coastal Administration, NOAA and Holfuy.
