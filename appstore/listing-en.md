## Description (max 4000)

Stop checking the forecast. Kookout tells you when and where it's good.

Pick your spots and tell Kookout what works at each one: the wind directions, how much wind, how big the waves, how much fresh snow. Kookout watches the forecast for you. When good conditions come up, you get a notification. When they move or disappear, you hear about that too.

FOR WIND, WAVES AND SNOW
• Wing foiling, kitesurfing, windsurfing: wind strength and the directions that work
• Surfing: wave height together with the right wind
• Skiing: powder days, counting the snow of the day and the day before

EVERY SPOT HAS ITS OWN RULES
• An onshore wind is great at one beach and useless at the next. Kookout judges each spot on its own terms.
• Home spots and weekend trips: Kookout tells you about your local spot any day, and about the one three hours away only when it's good on a day off, including public holidays.
• Start with popular spots near you, or drop a pin anywhere in the world.
• See the live wind from a weather station nearby before you head out.

NOTIFICATIONS WORTH GETTING
• A swoosh when it's on, a gong when it's off.
• Nothing between 22:00 and 07:00.
• You choose how far ahead to look.

BUILT WITH OTHER RIDERS
• Follow spots shared by others, and help keep names and positions right.
• Your rules stay yours. A spot's suggested rules come from what most of its riders use.
• No account needed. Sign in with Apple only if you want to share spots.

SIMPLE ON PURPOSE
No swell charts to decode. Kookout gives you the answer: go, or don't.

FREE FOR FOUNDING USERS
The first 2,000 users use Kookout for free. If that ever has to change, they'll hear about it well in advance.

Made in Norway by a wing foiler. Forecasts from MET Norway (yr.no), Kystverket, NOAA and Holfuy.
