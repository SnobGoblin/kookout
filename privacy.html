Privacy and support

Last updated 13 October 2026. This page covers the Kookout iPhone app and the web page at kookout.no.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to hi@kookout.no. We usually answer within a few days.

  • No notifications? Check that notifications for Kookout are allowed in the iPhone's Settings, that the spot has Notifications switched on (Edit), and use "Send a test notification" in Kookout's Settings. Nothing is sent between 22 and 07.
  • A spot is never good? Open it, tap Edit and check the wind directions and limits.
  • Start over: Settings → Start over removes your spots and settings on the phone.

In short

  • No ads, no tracking and no analytics. You only need an account (Sign in with Apple) to share spots and edit shared spots.
  • The app keeps your spots and settings on your phone. Copies are also kept on Kookout's server for notifications (on unless you switch them off: the spots with notifications on, with your phone's settings) and for sharing (only if you say yes when the app asks, the first time you make a spot: the spots you made, so others can find them). Notifications aren't linked to your name, email or Apple ID; shared spots are linked to your Kookout account.
  • The web page keeps your spots in your browser's local storage. It works like a cookie but is never sent to us, and the page sets no cookies.
  • To show forecasts, the location of your spots is sent to Kookout's server and the weather services listed below.

The iPhone app

Your spots and settings

Your spots (name, position and rules) and settings are stored on your iPhone. To work out a forecast, the app sends the spot's position and rules to Kookout's server, which asks the weather services and sends the answer back; that request isn't kept. Copies of your spots are kept on the server only for notifications (on unless you switch them off, so for most people most spots are on the server) and for sharing, if you choose it. Both are described below.

Your location

Kookout asks for your location only to find spots near you and to show where you are on the map. Your location stays on the phone, except that the middle of the map (or your position, when the map is zoomed far out), rounded to about 1 km, is sent to Kookout's server to find spots other people shared nearby. It isn't stored. Place searches go to Apple Maps.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Kookout's server stores for your phone: a random number the app makes up, the address Apple gives the app for sending notifications (a "push token"), the spots that have notifications on (name, position and rules), the app's language, units, clock and time zone, how far ahead to look, and which good periods it last told you about. It's used only to check your spots every hour and send notifications through Apple's push service. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID. It's deleted when you switch notifications off for all spots or for Kookout in the iPhone's Settings (the next time you open the app), and when Apple tells us the app was removed from the phone.

Your account

To share spots, or edit spots others share, you sign in with Apple. Kookout gets Apple's id for you (the same every time you sign in to Kookout, different for every app) and, the first time, your first name. You can change the name; it's shown on the spots you share and next to changes you make to shared spots. Kookout doesn't ask for your email address. The app keeps a session key in the iPhone's Keychain. To revoke the sign-in when you delete the account, Kookout's server also keeps a token from Apple.

Deleting your account: Settings → Sharing → Delete account. It deletes your account and sessions, and tells Apple to revoke Kookout's sign-in. Spots you shared stay for the people who use them, without your name. Your spots on the phone stay too. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for Kookout in the iPhone's Settings → Apple account → Sign in with Apple; the app then signs out.

Community spots

Shared spots, and the spots that come with the app, are community spots: anyone signed in can fix their name and position, and every change is kept in the spot's history with the time and the public name of whoever made it. Changes can be undone. Each spot's wind directions and limits are the majority of the versions its followers use, and its wind station for "Measured now" is the one most of its followers chose.

So that this works without an account, the app tells Kookout's server which community spots are in your list, and your own wind directions and limits for them if you changed them, and the wind station you picked for them (if not "Automatic"). It uses a random key the app makes, stored on the server only as a scrambled (hashed) value; it isn't linked to your account or your notifications. When you remove a spot, you stop following it. Spots nobody follows any more disappear from the map.

Reports

You can report a community spot, or a change someone made to it. Kookout stores the report (the spot, the change if any, the reason and your note, your account if you're signed in, and the app's random key) and reads it, by email to hi@kookout.no. Reports are deleted when they've been dealt with.

Shared spots

The first time you make a spot, the app asks whether to share your spots with other Kookout users, so they can find them on the map, and if so, which name to show. Your answer applies to the spots you make from then on; change it in Settings → Sharing, or for one spot with "Share with others" under Edit. Sharing needs an account (above). What's shared: the spot's name, position, time zone and rules, and your public name, shown as "Added by …". Shared spots are linked to your account, but not to your notifications. Switching sharing off, or deleting the spot, removes it from the server. Spots that came with the app or from other people aren't shared again.

The web page

Your spots (name, position and rules), the chosen units and the last spot you looked at are stored in your browser's local storage ("localStorage"). Unlike a cookie, it isn't sent to us with each visit, and the page sets no cookies. Clear the site data for kookout.no to remove them. The page asks for your location only if you tap "Bruk min posisjon" (use my location) when adding a spot.

Services Kookout uses

To show forecasts and live wind, the app, the page or Kookout's server contacts these services. Like any request on the internet, they see the IP address it comes from, along with the spot's position or your search.

  • Kookout's server, on Cloudflare (Workers and its database): forecasts, notifications, accounts, community spots and reports, as described above. Reports are emailed to Kookout through Resend. Cloudflare handles technical traffic data under its own privacy policy.
  • Apple: notifications (Apple Push Notification service), maps and place search in the app.
  • MET Norway (api.met.no, frost.met.no): weather and wave forecasts, wind observations.
  • The Norwegian Coastal Administration (Kystvær) and NOAA (weather service, buoys, WaveWatch III via PacIOOS and CoastWatch): wind observations and wave forecasts.
  • Holfuy: only if you open "See wind now" for a Holfuy station on the web page.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim and map tiles), jsDelivr and cdnjs: search, map and icons on the web page.

Your rights

You can ask what we store about you, or ask us to delete it, at hi@kookout.no. Since nothing is linked to your name, tell us which shared spot you mean, or simply delete it in the app. Kookout follows the GDPR; you can also contact the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
